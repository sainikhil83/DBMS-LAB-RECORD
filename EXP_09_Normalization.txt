DBMS MODEL LAB - EXPERIMENT 9
NORMALIZATION USING FUNCTIONAL DEPENDENCIES

AIM:
To study normalization up to 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, 4NF and 5NF.

ALGORITHM:
1. Identify attributes and functional dependencies.
2. Convert the relation into 1NF by removing multi-valued attributes.
3. Convert 1NF into 2NF by removing partial dependencies.
4. Convert 2NF into 3NF by removing transitive dependencies.
5. Remove multi-valued and join dependencies for 4NF and 5NF.

UNNORMALIZED:
SID | NAME | COURSE | PHONE
101 | RAVI | CSE, AI | 9876
102 | RAJU | CSE | 8765
103 | SITA | AI | 7654

1NF:
SID | NAME | COURSE | PHONE
101 | RAVI | CSE | 9876
101 | RAVI | AI | 9876
102 | RAJU | CSE | 8765
103 | SITA | AI | 7654

2NF:
STUDENT(SID, NAME, PHONE)
STUDENT_COURSE(SID, COURSE)

3NF:
SID -> NAME
SID -> PHONE

4NF:
STUDENT_COURSE(SID, COURSE)
STUDENT_PHONE(SID, PHONE)

5NF:
Remove unnecessary join dependencies and decompose relations so joins do not create incorrect information.

MEMORY:
1NF -> Atomic values
2NF -> No partial dependency
3NF -> No transitive dependency
4NF -> No multi-valued dependency
5NF -> No join dependency

RESULT:
The relation was successfully normalized up to 5NF.
