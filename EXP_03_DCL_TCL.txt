DBMS MODEL LAB - EXPERIMENT 3
DCL AND TCL COMMANDS

AIM:
To perform DCL and TCL commands.

DCL:
GRANT SELECT ON STUDENT TO SYSTEM;
REVOKE SELECT ON STUDENT FROM SYSTEM;

TCL:
UPDATE STUDENT SET MARKS=95 WHERE ROLLNO=101;
SAVEPOINT A;
ROLLBACK TO A;
COMMIT;

RESULT:
DCL and TCL commands were executed successfully.
